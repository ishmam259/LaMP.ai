# LaMP demo: voiceover script

Written for `LaMP-demo-2min.mp4` (2:21). Times are when each scene starts in the film. Speak at an easy pace, about 140 words a minute. Every line fits its scene with a second or two to spare. Say "taka" for BDT.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Today | This is Green Life Pharmacy in Bogura. The Today section of the app warns that the shop will be nineteen thousand taka short on 26 November, when Supplier A's order is due. |
| 0:11 | Cash | The Cash section lists every payment coming up. Supplier A's sixty-thousand-taka order on 26 November takes the shop below zero, and rent and salaries follow. |
| 0:25 | Scan (camera) | A supplier's bill arrives on paper. The owner opens the Scan section and takes a photo of it. LaMP reads every line: product, batch, expiry date and price. The total matches the bill, so the owner saves it. |
| 0:47 | Stock | The Stock section shows over eighteen thousand taka of medicine expiring in the next sixty days, and half of it can go back to suppliers. |
| 0:58 | Plan | The Plan section finds the cheapest way through: order fewer Montelukast boxes, then ask Supplier A for twenty more days. Borrowing it all would cost about four hundred taka. This plan costs nothing. |
| 1:15 | Ask LaMP | In the Ask LaMP section, the owner can just ask: "Can I pay Supplier A's order next week?" LaMP answers from the shop's own figures. It suggests steps, but never approves, pays or contacts anyone. |
| 1:31 | Approvals | Every step waits in the Approvals section, and nothing happens until the owner says yes. Each approval shows what it saved: over eleven thousand taka so far, mostly by not buying stock that would sit unsold. |
| 1:50 | Customers | The Customers section shows six families owing thirty-eight thousand taka. They're listed by number, never by name, and each has a link to their own card. |
| 2:03 | Customer's card | With that link, a customer opens their own card, sees they owe six thousand five hundred taka, and picks four weekly payments. Cards show amounts only, never names or medicines. |

About 260 words, roughly 110 seconds of speech in a 140-second film.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room with the phone close to your mouth. Do one take per row, so you can redo a single line.
- Leave half a second of silence at the start and end of each line.
- Send the audio as one file, or one file per row named `01.m4a`, `02.m4a`, and so on. It can then be cleaned and laid onto the film at these times.
