# LaMP demo: voiceover script

Written for `LaMP-demo-2min.mp4` (2:20). Times are when each scene starts in the film. Speak at an easy pace, about 140 words a minute. Every line fits its scene with a second or two to spare. Say "taka" for BDT.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Today | Green Life Pharmacy, Bogura. Today warns that the shop will be nineteen thousand taka short on 26 October, when Supplier A's order is due. |
| 0:11 | Cash | Cash lists every payment coming up. Supplier A's sixty-thousand-taka order on 26 October takes the shop below zero, and rent and salaries follow. |
| 0:27 | Scan (camera) | A supplier's bill arrives on paper. The owner opens Scan and takes a photo of it. LaMP reads every line: product, batch, expiry date and price. The total matches the bill, so the owner saves it. |
| 0:48 | Stock | Stock shows eighteen thousand four hundred taka of medicine expiring in the next sixty days, and half of it can go back to suppliers. |
| 0:59 | Plan | Plan finds the cheapest way through: order fewer Montelukast boxes, then ask Supplier A for twenty more days. Borrowing it all would cost four hundred and twenty-two taka. This plan costs nothing. |
| 1:16 | Ask LaMP | The owner can just ask: "Can I pay Supplier A's order next week?" LaMP answers from the shop's own figures. It suggests steps, but never approves, pays or contacts anyone. |
| 1:31 | Approvals | Every step waits here, and nothing happens until the owner says yes. Each approval shows what it saved: over eleven thousand taka so far, mostly by not buying stock that would sit unsold. |
| 1:51 | Customers | Six families owe thirty-eight thousand taka. They're listed by number, never by name, and each has a link to their own card. |
| 2:03 | Customer's card | With that link, a customer opens their own card, sees they owe six thousand five hundred taka, and picks four weekly payments. Cards show amounts only, never names or medicines. |

About 260 words, roughly 110 seconds of speech in a 140-second film.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room with the phone close to your mouth. Do one take per row, so you can redo a single line.
- Leave half a second of silence at the start and end of each line.
- Send the audio as one file, or one file per row named `01.m4a`, `02.m4a`, and so on. It can then be cleaned and laid onto the film at these times.
