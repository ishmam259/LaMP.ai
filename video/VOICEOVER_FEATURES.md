# LaMP phone demo: voiceover script

Written for `LaMP-features-demo.mp4` (3:19, on a phone, no intro). Times are when each scene starts in the film. Speak at an easy pace, about 140 words a minute; every line fits its scene with a little room to spare. Say "taka" for BDT.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Today | This is Green Life Pharmacy in Bogura. Today warns that the shop will be nineteen thousand taka short on the 26th of October, when Supplier A's order is due. |
| 0:13 | Cash | Cash shows the next thirty days: thirty-nine thousand taka today, and every bill coming up, so the owner sees the exact day the money runs out. |
| 0:26 | Scan (PDF bill) | Bills often arrive as PDFs. The owner picks one, and LaMP reads every line: product, batch, expiry and price. The total matches the bill, and the owner saves it. |
| 0:40 | Stock | Stock shows over eighteen thousand taka of medicine expiring soon, and half of it can go back. And twelve of the thirty Montelukast boxes on order won't sell in time. |
| 0:54 | Plan | Plan works out what to do, cheapest first. Order fewer Montelukast boxes and save ten thousand eight hundred taka. Ask Supplier A for twenty more days. Only if they say no, take a small mobile-wallet loan. Borrowing it all would cost 422 taka. This plan costs nothing. |
| 1:18 | Ask LaMP | The owner can just ask: "Can I pay Supplier A's order next week?" LaMP answers from the shop's own figures. It can suggest steps, but it never approves, pays or contacts anyone. |
| 1:34 | Credit record | The credit record: 38 of 41 supplier bills paid on time. The owner chooses which lender can see it. |
| 1:44 | Approvals | Every step waits in Approvals, and nothing happens until the owner says yes. Each approval shows what it saved: over eleven thousand taka so far, mostly by not buying stock that would expire on the shelf. |
| 2:07 | Customer's card | Families buy on credit too. With the code on their slip, a customer opens their own card on their phone. They owe six thousand five hundred taka, and choose four weekly payments. |
| 2:24 | Customers | Back at the counter, the owner sees the new plan, and can copy any customer's card link to send them. Cards show amounts only, never names or medicines. |
| 2:36 | Wallet | Wallet checks mobile-wallet payments against the bills. It spots a supplier paid twice and a payment with no bill, so the owner can ask for the money back. |
| 2:50 | Rules | Rules: the owner can pause LaMP at any time, and every step needs approval. |
| 2:57 | History | History keeps every step, and shows if anyone changes it later. |
| 3:04 | Lender's page | And the lender's side: Wallet Lender sees the shop's record and lends eight thousand two hundred taka, not nineteen thousand, because LaMP asks only for what the shop really needs. |

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room, phone close to your mouth, one take per row so you can redo a single line.
- Leave half a second of silence at the start and end of each line.
- Send the audio (one file, or one per row named `01.m4a`, `02.m4a`, …) and it can be laid onto the film at these times.
