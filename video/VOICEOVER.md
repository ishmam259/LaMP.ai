# LaMP demo: voiceover script

Matches `video/LaMP-AIgnite-demo.mp4` (1:54). Times are when each scene starts in the film. Speak at an easy pace, about 150 words a minute; every line fits its scene with a little room to spare. Say "taka" for BDT.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room, phone close to your mouth, one take per row so you can redo a single line.
- Leave half a second of silence at the start and end of each line.
- Send the audio (one file, or one per row named `01.m4a`, `02.m4a`, …) and it can be laid onto the film at these times.
